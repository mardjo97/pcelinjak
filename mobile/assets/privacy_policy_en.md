# Privacy policy – Pčelinjak

**Last updated: 15 August 2026. · [Account deletion](https://hexatech.rs/account-deletion/pcelinjak)**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Pčelinjak** app (“app”, “we”) collects, uses and protects your data. By using the app you accept these terms.

The app is developed by Hexatech. For privacy questions use the contact below.

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 Data you provide

- **Account:** email, first name, last name, phone (optional) and a password hash. Only one device can be active per account (device identifier).
- **Apiary records:** hives, queens, notes, harvests, work groups and reminders. Stored locally and, when you sync, on our server linked to your account.
- **Feedback:** if you send feedback, the message and email are stored to improve the app.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

- **Technical data and logs:** device type, OS, app version, IP address and request time — to run the service and fix errors, without marketing profiling.
- **Push notifications:** a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token. The app sends it to our backend; we use it only for reminders you configured. We do not use it for ads.

### 2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not sell your data. We do not collect background location, track you for advertising, or share data with third parties for marketing. The app has no ads.

## 3. Purpose

- **Provide the service:** account, sync, reminders.
- **Email:** account activation and similar service messages from noreply@hexatech.rs.
- **Security:** account protection; one active device per account.
- **Legal obligations** and fixing errors.

## 4. Legal basis

- **Contract performance:** processing needed for the service you accepted.
- **Consent:** where required (e.g. device notifications).
- **Legitimate interest:** security and operating the service.

## 5. Retention

- Servers are in Europe (Hetzner, Germany).
- We keep data while the account is active and as required by law after deletion.
- Passwords are not stored in readable form. Traffic uses HTTPS.

## 6. Sharing

- **Hosting:** Hetzner (servers and database) — processing only on our behalf.
- **Email:** SMTP (Loopia) for service messages.
- **Google:** Google Play distribution; FCM for reminder push.
- **Law:** disclosure if required by a competent authority.

We do not sell data for marketing.

## 7. Your rights

Under applicable law (including GDPR where it applies) you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, object to processing, or lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Serbia). Email the contact below; we respond within the time required by law (typically within 30 days).

## 8. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures (encryption in transit, password hashing, limited access) to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

## 9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 16. If we learn we collected a child’s data without appropriate consent, we will delete it.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change. We will notify you of significant changes in the app or by email.
